Walkthrough

Fixing the tight week

Follow these steps with the tight-week practice file open. It takes about ten minutes.

  1. Check the school day

    After the import you land on School day. Hillview runs Monday to Saturday, with seven 40-minute periods, a break after period 3 and lunch after period 5. You can rebuild the bells with Quick build at the bottom of the page.

  2. Read the lessons table

    Open Lessons. The form at the top adds lessons. Pick the classes, the subject, the teachers and how many a week. If you pick several classes, it adds one lesson for each class. Tick Joint lesson when those classes are taught together, like PE here.

    Use the filter on the right of All lessons to show one class. In 10B, look at Computer Science: it is set to Triples. That is the first mistake.

    On a smaller screen each lesson shows as a card with the same boxes, instead of a row.

  3. Look at the rules

    Open Availability. Choose a teacher, class, room or subject, then click boxes to mark times as Off. Click a day or period name to change a whole row or column.

    The tight week has a rule on each tab:

    • Teachers: Sana Javed (Chemistry) only works Monday to Wednesday. That is the second mistake: she has more lessons than free periods.
    • Classes: every class is off on Saturday periods 5 to 7, which makes Saturday a half day.
    • Rooms: the Computer Lab is used by the junior school every morning.
    • Subjects: PE is never in periods 6 and 7, because it's too hot outside after lunch.

  4. Read the pre-check, then create

    Open Create timetable. Before you press anything, the Pre-check compares how many lessons each teacher, class and room needs with how many periods they have free.

    Red means something can't fit. Amber means it's tight but should work. Red doesn't stop you: press Create timetable anyway to see what happens.

  5. Fix the two problems

    Fix 1: Computer Science for 10B

    In Lessons, filter to 10B and change Computer Science from Triples to Doubles. A block of three only fits in periods 1 to 3, because doubles and triples never run across a break. Those are exactly the hours the Computer Lab is busy.

    Fix 2: Sana Javed's days

    In Availability, choose Sana Javed and click the words Thursday and Friday to make both days free.

    Go back to Create timetable and press Create again.

  6. Check the timetable and move lessons by hand

    Open View & print. Switch between Classes, Teachers and Rooms, and use Previous and Next to page through them. Striped boxes are times that are off, like Saturday afternoon.

    To move a lesson, drag it, or tap it and then tap a box. While you move it, every box shows whether the lesson can go there:

    Green free Amber taken, but you can swap or make room Red not allowed
    Lock or fix?

    Lock keeps a lesson in place when you create the timetable again. Unlock all and Clear unlocked lessons release locks. Fix to this slot is stronger: nothing moves a fixed lesson, not even Clear all. Use it for things that never change, like assembly.

  7. Print and export

    The buttons at the top of View & print:

    ExcelCSVCSV (this view)Print / PDFPrint all classesDownload backup
    • Excel makes one sheet for every class, teacher and room. Excel may ask you to confirm before opening the file. Choose Yes.
    • Print / PDF prints what's on screen. To make a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
    • Print all classes prints every class on its own page. On the Teachers tab it prints every teacher.
    • Overview shows all classes on one sheet, good for the staffroom wall.

  8. Check workloads

    Reports shows each teacher's periods per day, gaps and longest run without a break, and whether every class got all its lessons. You can download it as CSV.

Good to know

Things that catch people out

Create again or try another arrangement?

Create again gives the same result unless you changed something. Try another arrangement shuffles and gives a different timetable. Both keep locked and fixed lessons.

Some changes remove placed lessons

Changing a lesson's "Lessons a week" or "Arrange as" sends that lesson back to Unscheduled. Changing the number of days, periods or breaks clears the whole timetable.

Six periods a day is the limit

By default no teacher teaches more than 6 periods a day. A part-time teacher who works three days can take at most 18. Change it per teacher on the Teachers page, or for everyone under Rules.

"Most in a day" is per lesson

It stops the same lesson landing twice on one day. If a class has more lessons of a subject than there are days, set it to 2.

Several classes, one lesson each

Picking several classes in the form adds a separate lesson for each. Tick "Joint lesson" only when the classes sit together with the same teacher.

Some fields are only for your records

Class teacher, number of students and room capacity are kept and printed, but the timetable doesn't use them when placing lessons.

Where are the reasons?

The reasons for unplaced lessons are on Create timetable. On View & print, hold the mouse over a card in Unscheduled to see its reason.

Undo works everywhere

Use the Undo button at the bottom of the menu, or Ctrl+Z. It goes back up to 40 changes.

Saving

Keeping your work safe

Your timetable saves by itself, but only in this browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Clearing your browser history, or using a private window, removes it.

Press Download backup after any big change. The backup is one small file. To carry on at another computer, open TimetableSama there and use Import backup… on My timetables. An amber reminder at the top of the page appears until you've made a backup.

You can keep several timetables side by side, for example a draft and a final version. Use Duplicate, Rename and the "Timetable you are working on" list in the menu.

Coming from TimetableMaster

TimetableSama follows the same steps, so most of what you know carries over. These lists compare it with TimetableMaster's own help pages as of October 2026.

The same

  • The same stepsBell schedule, subjects, teachers, classes, rooms, lessons, generate, adjust, export.
  • Short codes and pasting listsPaste names straight from a spreadsheet.
  • Combined classes and team teachingSeveral classes or several teachers on one lesson. Singles, doubles and triples.
  • Time offFor teachers, classes, subjects and rooms.
  • A check before generatingPlus a list of lessons that didn't fit.
  • Drag and dropWith clash checks, swapping and undo.
  • Excel and PDFFree here. They are paid features on TimetableMaster.

Done differently

  • Shorter daysOne bell schedule for every day. For a short Friday or Saturday, mark the last periods Off for every class, as the tight week does.
  • Week A and Week BAdd the days twice, named "A Mon", "B Mon" and so on.
  • Mixed lengths in one lessonLeftovers are automatic: 5 as doubles gives 2 + 2 + 1. For other mixes, add two lesson rows.
  • SavingIn your browser instead of online. Share by sending the backup file.
  • PDFMade from the print window with "Save as PDF".
  • Rules you can tuneDaily limits, gaps and spreading have their own settings.

Not here yet

  • Splitting a class into groupsFor example Biology and Computer electives at the same time, or half-class labs.
  • Staff logins and sharingNo accounts, roles or shared editing.
  • Substitutions and leaveNo cover planning, absence or school calendar.
  • Apps and importsNo mobile app, email sharing, or import from other timetable programs.
